# Current Authoritative Flyer Specification

This document consolidates the latest explicit instructions. Where the record
contains uncertainty, it is marked rather than silently resolved.

## Page and hierarchy

- Portrait US Letter: 8.5 by 11 inches.
- Dark-gray or neutral border suitable for ordinary printing.
- The entrance photograph is the main visual anchor.
- The entrance/bus/parking guide is a substantial lower section rather than a
  thin footer.
- Detailed text must be large enough to print clearly, even when a controlled
  text panel overlaps part of the entrance photograph.

## Header image and placement

- Use a tight crop of the original engraved First Step Beginners sign.
- Level the lettering horizontally.
- Position the sign in the upper-left area, over the surrounding image if
  necessary, but do not cover the door.
- It should be substantially larger than the small-sign draft—approximately
  twice that earlier width and height was requested.
- It does not need to look like a separate church sign.
- Do not add separate white “THE FIRST STEP BEGINNERS” text because the wooden
  sign already communicates the name.
- “RECOVERY, UNITY, AND SERVICE” may be refactored into or around the AA logo in
  the header rather than placed in the footer.

## Output and quality checks

- Canonical editable source: SVG or HTML/CSS.
- Print-ready PDF: exact US Letter page.
- PNG proof: 2550 by 3300 pixels at 300 DPI.
- No clipped text, AI-generated misspellings, warped lettering, or invented
  map/entrance details.
- Check readability on a physical 8.5-by-11-inch print, not only when zoomed on
  screen.
