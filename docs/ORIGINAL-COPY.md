It is historical evidence only. `CURRENT-SPEC.md` controls the next revision.

## Original middle copy

> The First Step Beginners AA Group holds a meeting at Trinity UMC in Downtown
> Durham, NC designed to be equally welcoming for old-time AA members, AA
> newcomers, and it is also adjacent to The Open Table Ministry’s Free Store and
> Resource Weekly Event. This was done purposely as we hope to also be welcoming
> and accessible to the population that frequents the Open Table, a population
> that is often experiencing Housing Insecurity and Homelessness.
>
> This AA group is an opportunity to by just simply attending an AA meeting, do
> service work that benefits yourself, others in attendance in AA, and
> potentially those who enter AA from the population coming to the Open Table.
>
> The First Step Beginners AA Group had decided to theme these topical discussion
> meetings on the initial steps of AA, with each topic being anchored in official
> AA literature: The first Tuesday of the month will have a First Step theme. The
> second Tuesday: a Second Step theme, The third Tuesday will be a Third Step
> theme. The fourth Tuesday will be Fourth Step theme. If there exists a fifth
> Tuesday of the month then The First Step Beginners Group will select a home
> group member to tell their story for 20 minutes, followed by a topical
> discussion.

## Removed material retained for provenance

- The separate overlay name “THE FIRST STEP BEGINNERS” was removed because the
  photographed sign already gives the group name.
- The service-work paragraph was removed to enlarge the remaining copy.
- The detailed first-, second-, third-, fourth-, and fifth-Tuesday format was
  replaced with one sentence about discussing the first four steps using
  official AA literature.
- The “brand new” line and the September 8 starting-date parenthetical were
  removed.
- “RECOVERY, UNITY, AND SERVICE” was removed from the footer and considered for
  placement near the header AA logo.
